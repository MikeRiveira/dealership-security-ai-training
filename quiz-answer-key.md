# Quiz Answer Key

Every video pauses 3 seconds for each question, then reveals the answer on screen.

| Module | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A "lender" emails new bank details for a payoff. What should you do? | C. Call the lender at a number already on file |
| 1 | Your "GM" texts from an unknown number asking for gift card codes. This is… | B. A scam: verify it and report it |
| 2 | Your phone asks you to approve a sign-in you didn't start. You should… | C. Deny it and report it to IT |
| 2 | A coworker asks to borrow your DMS login "for just a minute." You… | C. Say no, and point them to IT |
| 3 | You need a copy of a customer's driver's license. Best way? | C. Scan it into the approved system |
| 3 | A customer asks you to delete all their data. You… | C. Write it down and pass it to your manager |
| 4 | A ransom note pops up on your screen. First step? | C. Disconnect from the network and call IT |
| 4 | When must the FTC be notified of a breach affecting 500+ customers? | A. Within 30 days of discovery |
| 5 | Which is OK to include when an approved AI tool drafts a follow-up email? | B. The vehicle, trim, and features they liked |
| 5 | An AI tool says there's a $3,000 rebate this month. You should… | B. Verify it with official manufacturer sources first |
| 6 | A website tool sets prices for NY shoppers using their personal data. What's required? | B. The notice "This price was set by an algorithm using your personal data." |
| 6 | Marketing wants an AI-generated "person" in a social ad seen in NY. What's required? | B. A conspicuous synthetic performer disclosure |
| 6 | A vendor's AI hiring tool screens out applicants unfairly in NJ. Who's responsible? | C. The employer |
| 7 | The SSN on a credit app was issued before the applicant was born. You should… | B. Pause, verify, and escalate to a manager |
| 7 | Can you pull a friend's credit "just to see" if they'd qualify? | C. No. Credit pulls need a permissible purpose and authorization |
| 7 | A customer replies STOP to a promo text. You… | B. Honor it and stop marketing texts to them |

**AI Compliance Masterclass:** the five questions repeat the Module 5 and 6 answers, plus one new one: "Your AI email tool writes GUARANTEED approval for everyone." Answer: B, edit it. Ads can't be deceptive no matter who writes them.
