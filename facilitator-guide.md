# Facilitator Guide

## Overview

Seven narrated MP4 videos (4:53 to 7:03 each, about 42 minutes total) train every department on IT security and responsible AI use, with New York, New Jersey and federal rules built in. Each video ends with 2–3 on-screen quiz questions and has a matching `.srt` caption file.

**Audience:** all employees in all departments, at every store.

**Cast:** Byte (a wisecracking cyber-safety mascot car) narrates with car puns and light jokes. Sam (Sales), Joe (F&I), Rosa (Service), Mike (Accounting), Tony (GM) and Anthony (Marketing) act out real dealership scenarios. All characters and the lender "Capital Motor Credit" are invented.

## Suggested rollout

1. Have counsel review the [legal references](legal-references.md) and the checklist at the end of this guide.
2. Upload the MP4s and `.srt` captions to your LMS, SharePoint or a shared drive.
3. Assign Modules 1–4 (security) in week 1 and Modules 5–7 (AI and federal rules) in week 2.
4. Collect a signed [acknowledgment](acknowledgment-form.md) per employee and keep it on file. The FTC Safeguards Rule expects documented security awareness training.
5. Repeat annually, and refresh Module 6 whenever NY or NJ AI laws change.

## Module details

### 1. Don't Take the Bait
- **Scenarios:** fake lender portal email (Sales); gift-card text from the "GM" (Service); changed payoff wire instructions (Accounting); fake DMS support call asking for an MFA code (F&I).
- **Takeaways:** five red flags; verify through a known number; two-person approval for wires; report to IT, no blame.

### 2. Lock It Down
- **Scenarios:** shared sales-desk login on a sticky note; MFA fatigue push spam at 11 PM; unlocked screen in the showroom.
- **Takeaways:** passphrases of 14+ characters; never share logins or codes; deny unexpected MFA prompts; lock with Win+L; never plug in found USB drives; NY and NJ employee monitoring notices.

### 3. Protect the Deal Jacket
- **Scenarios:** credit app contents; license photo texted from a personal phone; customer asks to delete their data.
- **Takeaways:** FTC Safeguards Rule; what NY and NJ define as protected info; clean desk and shredding; reset infotainment systems on trade-ins; route NJ Data Privacy Act requests to management; employee SSN rules.

### 4. Code Red
- **Scenarios:** the CDK Global ransomware attack (June 2024); a ransom note on a PC.
- **Takeaways:** disconnect, don't power off, call IT; warning signs to report; NY 30-day, NJ State Police-first and FTC 30-day notice clocks; no social media or outside talk.

### 5. AI: Smart & Safe
- **Scenarios:** credit details pasted into a free chatbot; AI invents a warranty and rebate; an AI voice clone of the GM requests a wire.
- **Takeaways:** approved tools only; no customer or confidential data in unapproved AI; verify outputs; no fake reviews; no AI in credit, pricing or hiring decisions without approval.

### 6. AI & the Law
- **Scenarios:** personalized website pricing; AI spokesperson in a social ad; AI voice bots; AI hiring tools; F&I fairness.
- **Takeaways:** NY algorithmic pricing disclosure; NY synthetic performer disclosure; NJ deepfake law and NY right of publicity; TCPA and AI voices; NJ LAD, NYC Local Law 144 and NYSHRL; ECOA; NJDPA profiling opt-out.

### 7. Federal Rules of the Road
- **Scenarios:** ID and credit app that don't match (F&I); credit pull on a relative "just to see" (Sales); deceptive email blast subject line (Marketing); promo texts (Service).
- **Takeaways:** FTC Red Flags Rule and Address Discrepancy Rule; FCRA permissible purpose, adverse action notices and the Disposal Rule; CAN-SPAM; TCPA consent, STOP opt-outs and 8 a.m.–9 p.m. calling hours; federal AI status as of 2026; follow the stricter law.

### Standalone: AI Compliance Masterclass (14:45)
One combined video for AI-focused assignments.
- **Part 1:** smart AI habits (from Module 5).
- **Part 2:** New York and New Jersey AI law (from Module 6), plus the federal TAKE IT DOWN Act on intimate deepfakes.
- **Part 3:** federal rules: deceptive AI-written ads under the FTC Act and CAN-SPAM, AI in credit decisions under ECOA/Regulation B and the FCRA, and the federal AI status from Module 7.

It ends with a 7-point checklist and 5 quiz questions (see the [answer key](quiz-answer-key.md)).

## Customize before rollout

The videos use generic wording where a dealership's internal process may differ. Confirm each item matches your policies:

- [ ] Counsel has reviewed the legal references (especially Module 6)
- [ ] Your IT help desk contact: employees forward phishing there; for ransomware they contact IT by phone, in person, or from a different device
- [ ] Two-person approval for wires and payoffs is your actual policy (Modules 1 and 5)
- [ ] Password guidance: 14+ character passphrases, and a company password manager if you provide one
- [ ] The ransomware step ("unplug the network cable, don't power off, call IT") matches your incident response plan
- [ ] HR has written NY Civil Rights Law § 52-c monitoring notices and acknowledgments for NY employees
- [ ] Privacy requests: the videos say "pass it to your manager"; name a specific contact if you have one
- [ ] An approved AI tools list exists and employees know where to find it
