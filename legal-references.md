# Legal References

The videos cite these laws as of September 2026. **This is training content, not legal advice.** Have your own counsel review before rollout.

## Federal

| Law | What the video says | Module |
|---|---|---|
| [FTC Safeguards Rule](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/automobile-dealers-ftcs-safeguards-rule-frequently-asked-questions) (16 CFR Part 314, GLBA) | Dealers that arrange financing are financial institutions; MFA, access controls and security training required; FTC notice within 30 days for breaches of 500+ consumers' unencrypted info | 2, 3, 4 |
| TCPA + FCC declaratory ruling (Feb. 2024) | AI-generated voices are "artificial" voices; marketing robocalls need prior express written consent | 6 |
| FTC Consumer Reviews & Testimonials Rule (2024) | Fake reviews, including AI-generated ones, are banned | 5, 6 |
| Equal Credit Opportunity Act | Fair lending applies with or without AI | 6 |
| FTC Red Flags Rule (16 CFR Part 681) + FACTA Address Discrepancy Rule | Dealers that pull credit need a written Identity Theft Prevention Program; spot red flags, pause, verify, escalate; confirm reports with address discrepancy notices | 7 |
| Fair Credit Reporting Act (incl. § 619) + FTC Disposal Rule | Pull credit only with a permissible purpose and authorization; false pretenses can mean fines and up to 2 years in prison; adverse action notices; shred reports | 7 |
| CAN-SPAM Act | Honest headers and subject lines; identify as an ad; mailing address; working unsubscribe honored within 10 business days; penalties can exceed $50,000 per email | 7 |
| TCPA texting rules + FCC consent-revocation rule (2025) | Texts count as calls; marketing texts need prior express written consent; honor opt-outs sent any reasonable way ("stop," "quit," "cancel"); no telemarketing before 8 a.m. or after 9 p.m. | 7 |
| Federal AI status (Dec. 2025 executive order) | No comprehensive federal AI law; task force to challenge state AI laws; NY and NJ laws remain in effect; existing federal laws apply to AI; follow the stricter law | 7 |

## New York

| Law | What the video says | Module |
|---|---|---|
| [Breach notification amendments](https://www.corporatecomplianceinsights.com/new-york-tightens-breach-clock/) (GBL § 899-aa, eff. Dec. 2024 / Mar. 2025) | Notify affected residents within 30 days; also the AG, Dept. of State and State Police; medical and health insurance info added | 3, 4 |
| SHIELD Act (GBL § 899-bb) | Reasonable safeguards, including employee training | 3 |
| Civil Rights Law § 52-c | Written notice and acknowledgment of electronic monitoring | 2 |
| Labor Law § 203-d | No public posting or display of employee SSNs | 3 |
| [Algorithmic Pricing Disclosure Act](https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2025/11/new-yorks-novel-algorithmic-pricing-disclosure-law-takes-effect) (eff. Nov. 10, 2025) | Exact notice "THIS PRICE WAS SET BY AN ALGORITHM USING YOUR PERSONAL DATA"; up to $1,000 per violation | 6 |
| [Synthetic performer ad disclosure](https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/synthetic-performers-real-consequences-implications-of-trailblazing-new-york-ai-ad-law) (eff. June 9, 2026) | Conspicuous disclosure for AI-generated people in ads; $1,000 first, $5,000 after | 6 |
| Civil Rights Law §§ 50–51 | No use of a person's name, picture or voice in ads without written consent | 6 |
| NYC Local Law 144; NYS Human Rights Law | Bias audit and notice for automated hiring tools in NYC; anti-discrimination applies to any tool | 6 |
